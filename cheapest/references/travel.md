# Flights and hotel stays

Search only the sections relevant to the mission. Provider names are discovery
routes, not installed integrations. Use the shared app/access guidance when a
route needs browser interaction, a session, or a small direct script.

## Flights and other transport fares

Establish origin/destination, allowed airports or stations, outbound and return
dates, flexibility, passenger count/ages, cabin, bags, and hard limits on times,
stops or duration. State assumptions for a test; do not silently relax a real
mission. City groups can include distant airports with extra transfer costs.

Start with Google Flights or a regional comparison search, then relevant airline
sites and booking agents such as Expedia, Skyscanner or KAYAK where accessible.
Discover low-cost carriers and operators omitted by one comparison service.
For rail/bus/ferry tickets, start with the actual operator and regional comparison
sites; verify class, reservation and baggage rules. A generic route page's fare
floor or calendar price is a lead until the requested journey is selected.

Select both legs before comparing round trips. Record flight/train identifiers,
operating carrier, local departure/arrival dates and times, airports/stations,
connections and overnight changes. Distinguish through tickets from separate
tickets/self-transfers: count baggage recheck, airport transfers, transit needs
and missed-connection exposure. Do not propose hidden-city ticketing as an
equivalent fare. Dates/airport changes require user acceptance when constrained.

Compare total for all passengers with taxes and required fees, the requested
bags on every leg, and any required seat selection or payment charges. Personal
item is not a cabin bag. Check fare family rather than guessing from the airline
or cabin name. Keep optional seats, checked bags, insurance and paid memberships
separate unless requested. Treat miles/points redemption as a separate scenario.

Follow the best leads to the airline/agent's review before personal or payment
submission. Verify itinerary, baggage, fare restrictions and precise total again;
the comparison may round dollars or quote a different fare. Record the actual
seller and its change/refund/support terms. A bag filter error means a failed
request: try the ordinary unfiltered search and inspect candidate allowances,
or declare missing verification. Do not interpret the error as no flights.

## Hotels

Establish destination or exact property/address, check-in and check-out dates,
night count, room count, adults/children and their ages, and requirements such
as private room/bathroom, bed type, accessibility, location or cancellation.
For a city-wide mission, discover multiple suitable properties before comparing
channels for promising ones. A single-property test proves only that scope.

Start with Google Hotels or a local comparison service, the property's official
booking link, and relevant OTAs such as Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda
or regional sellers. Follow cheaper suppliers surfaced by comparisons, including
discounters. Reconfirm property address, dates and occupancy after each handoff;
links can discard parameters, and changing occupancy can reset dates. Wait for
updated offers; a changed input beside stale prices is not a refreshed quote.

Compare the full stay for all rooms with taxes, mandatory property/resort/service
fees and charges due at the property. Check whether displayed rates are nightly,
average nightly, or full stay; “with fees” can still exclude taxes. Calendar
“from” rates may refer to different rooms and cannot be summed into a confirmed
stay. When card fees conflict with details, read the selected review/checkout
total and retain the discrepancy. Do not rank using a guessed nightly formula.
Show refundable security deposits separately as funds required, not a discount
or a consumed stay cost; leave unknown amounts unresolved.

Match room/bed type, meals, occupancy, payment timing, cancellation deadline and
timezone, refundability, membership and app-only eligibility. Cheaper nonrefundable
rates and refundable rates are different options. Quote the price premium and
actual terms so the user can judge value. Do not subtract future rewards or an
“up to” sale percentage from the already discounted rate.

Investigate split stays when useful, but disclose separate reservations, room or
property moves, gaps and deposits/fees per booking. They are not equivalent to
one continuous room. Verify all segments and combined cost before recommending
them as usable; do not book one segment merely to test the other. Package rates,
member offers and new-user promos remain conditional until eligibility and the
requested basket are verified. Never enroll or create holds during research.

## Cheapest and best deal

Lead with the lowest verified option meeting the hard constraints. Show a useful
alternative when a concrete premium buys flexibility, less travel time, better
location or no room change. Do not invent a weighted “value score.” Ask about a
tradeoff only if it changes the decision; otherwise report the options clearly.
Keep seller-listed leads separate from final review totals, and identify blocked
routes and unpriced challengers. Neither a successful handoff nor one tested
itinerary establishes universal cheapest coverage.
